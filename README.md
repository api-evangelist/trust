# Trust

Trust (Trust Software Inc., trust.co) was a fintech and marketing-technology startup founded in 2019 by James Borow and backed by a 2020 seed investment from Lightspeed Venture Partners. It began as a corporate card and community giving digital businesses higher credit limits and extended payment terms on marketing spend, then pivoted to Market (withmarket.com), an AI marketing assistant that let Shopify merchants chat with their business data.

As of July 2026 both trust.co and withmarket.com no longer resolve in DNS and the company appears to have wound down. No public API, developer portal, or SDK surface was ever published, so this profile carries no API artifacts.
